# 🪶 Tablet polish

## North star

A quiet, tactile museum page. One title, one mechanism, one action. Preserve the approved artistic Leo. Everything fits one tablet screen, with no scrolling or automatic demonstrations.

## Screenshot passes

1. Controls: the original step navigation was centred on the whole page, not the model. Replaced four disconnected numbered circles with one journey rail. Aligned the title, action and rail. Unified header, arrow and speech controls. Arabic sans-serif numerals remain easy to recognise. Every step target is at least 44 px.
2. Geometry: close-ups showed hidden side edges crossing gear tops. Paint concave gear side edges before the opaque top. Removed duplicate tooth-root vertices. Preserve the 12:24 involute pair, matching module and opposite 2:1 rotation. Check twelve phases and four 4× contact crops.
3. Composition: keep the gear board camera identical when the spring appears. Paint the supporting platform below its mounted parts, so it cannot hide the knight’s rear boot. Seat both calves on their boots. Move the faceplate and eyes in front of the helmet, and join the cart winding shaft to its spring axle. Strengthen secondary control outlines. Tighten the finished cart’s view to the measured bounds of its whole journey, so the machine is larger without clipping its travel.
